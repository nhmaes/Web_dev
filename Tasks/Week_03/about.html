About me I have a wide multidisciplanary background in various arts. From traditionnal to digital, I have experiemented with various mediums. From when I was a child up to when I was around 15-16 years old, I actually wanted to pursue fine arts (painting) and animation. Drawn to a passion for comics and graphic novels, I began trying to create my own. However, when It was time to chose my A-Level subjects, I ended up chosing Art, Communications and Computer Science. This matched all of my interests that stood on a spectrum between media and technology. Fascinated by how interactive the digital world is becoming, I looked into and researched different formats to how digital mediums can make an impact on the user, wether it be video-games, apps, movies, websites, etc...
After high-school I began my foundation year at University of the Arts London where I specialised in Communications at Central Saint Martins ( Archway ). Once I had secured my pathway in graphic design, I continued my studies for the rest of the academic year at the UAL Pre-Degree School in Lime Grove. Although I had developped a passionate interest towards graphic design, I wanted to still pursue a bachelors where I got to experiment with more techincal and digital tools. This is when I applied and got accepted into the Creative Computing Institute of UAL.
